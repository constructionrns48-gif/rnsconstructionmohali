About Us

RNS Construction Mohali - We Build Your Dream

A construction company, builder and contractor based in Mohali, Punjab - serving homeowners and businesses across Tricity.

RNS Construction Mohali is a construction company, builder and contractor based in Mohali, Punjab. We help homeowners and businesses with house construction, renovation and interior work across Mohali, Chandigarh, Zirakpur, Kharar, Panchkula, New Chandigarh, Sunny Enclave, Landran, Banur and nearby areas.

From houses, kothis, villas and independent floors to commercial buildings, showrooms, shops and offices, we handle construction and finishing under one roof. Our services also include home renovation, repairs, extensions and interior design, with false ceilings, flooring and painting.

For turnkey projects, we bring design, construction and interiors together through to key handover. We also carry out civil work, including foundations, RCC columns, boundary walls, and AAC block and brick walls.

Tell us about your plot and the space you want to build. Our team can discuss your design, materials and scope of work, arrange a free site visit and prepare a project-specific quotation.
