Is solar right for your roof?

Solar can cut an electricity bill, but it is not the right answer for every roof. Shade, roof strength, available area and how much power you use during the day decide whether it pays off. We would rather say that up front than sell something that does not fit.

If you are searching for solar panel installation in Mohali, send us your monthly bill and a few photos of the roof. That alone tells us a lot.

Homes, buildings and factories

We take enquiries for rooftop solar installation in Chandigarh and the Tricity for houses and apartments. For shops, offices and commercial solar panel installation, the sizing is based on daytime load. Factories and warehouses look at larger roof areas, and solar for factories and buildings in Punjab is something we can discuss once we know the roof type, the sanction load and the supply arrangement.

For solar panel installation in Punjab, the state's net metering rules and the local utility matter as much as the panels. Net metering, subsidy schemes and utility approvals change over time. They are checked at the time of your enquiry, not assumed.

How it works with us

RNS Construction does not install solar with its own crew. We coordinate partner solar installers, so you get one point of contact while the specialist work is done by teams who do this every day. We can also help with roof and structural points, since we are a construction company.

We do not make promises about savings or payback on this page. Real numbers come after a roof assessment.

Enquiries from across India

Our office is in Mohali, so Punjab, Chandigarh and the Tricity are the easiest for us to coordinate. Enquiries from other parts of India are welcome too. Send the location and scope, and we will tell you plainly whether we can arrange it and what has to be checked first.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I know what size of solar system I need?

It depends on your monthly consumption, roof area and budget. Send us a recent electricity bill and roof photos and we can start from there.

2. Do you provide subsidy or net metering help?

Schemes and rules change. We check what applies to your case at the time and tell you what documents are needed.

3. Can solar be installed on any roof?

Not always. Shade, roof condition and available area matter, and an assessment tells you honestly.

4. Do you install solar yourselves?

No. We coordinate partner solar installers and stay your point of contact.

5. Do you take solar projects outside Mohali?

We take enquiries from across India. Distance and local rules are reviewed first.
